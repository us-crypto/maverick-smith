Saffron

Free · Android now · iPhone next

A recipe app. Tell it how many people you cook for, your budget, your mood, your diet and the kitchen you actually have, and it suggests dishes you can really cook, with photos, ingredients, steps and a tutorial video. Twelve languages. No account, no tracking. Free to use; a small ad can be switched off for a day by choosing to watch one.
